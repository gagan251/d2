A letter for a difficult day 💌

Dear you,

If you are reading this, today was probably hard. I’m sorry.

I can’t fix it from here. But I want you to know a few things.

You don’t have to hold everything together. You are allowed to be tired, and annoyed, and a bit lost. That doesn’t make you weak. It makes you human.

I have seen how much you give to other people. How patient you are. How you keep going even when no one notices. I notice.

Today might feel heavy. But heavy days end. Tomorrow won’t be the same.

For now, drink some water, sit somewhere soft, and let that be enough.

I’m really glad you exist.

With care 🌷
